FICTIONAL SOFTWARE-ASSISTED PROCUREMENT AGREEMENT — DEMONSTRATION ONLY

Authorized Tools and Approval Requirements

Authorized users may use software tools to prepare purchase orders and vendor communications. Software tools may not make a binding commitment. Human approval is required before any commitment and before a transaction above USD 1,000.

Authority to Act

Only an identified human procurement manager is authorized to act on behalf of Fictional Acme Procurement Ltd when accepting terms or entering into an agreement.

Audit Log and Security

Detailed audit log records must be retained. Information security measures apply to personal data and confidential information processed through the procurement service.
