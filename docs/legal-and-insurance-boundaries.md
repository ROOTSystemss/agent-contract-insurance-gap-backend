# Legal and Insurance Boundaries

This product identifies review questions. It does not provide legal advice, insurance advice, coverage opinions, enforceability determinations, or guaranteed conclusions.

Findings must keep five layers separate: exact or absent document fact, customer-declared agent fact, system inference, versioned review rule, and neutral question for a qualified professional. Quoted evidence must be an exact substring of extracted source text. Absence findings contain no invented quotation and explicitly acknowledge missing documents, OCR limitations, and detector limitations.

The system must never state that the customer is covered or not covered, a contract is invalid or enforceable, an insurer must pay, or a clause has definitive legal effect. Standard policy wording is not copied. Country scaffolds are not presented as law. The canonical disclaimer in `src/shared/disclaimer.ts` is included in every finding and report.
